# Frequently Asked Questions

## Safety & Is This Tour Right for Me?

### How do you keep guests safe in the water?

Every tour includes a safety briefing before entering the water, and experienced safety divers stay with guests throughout the swim. The team closely watches shark behavior, ocean conditions, and the group so guests can focus on enjoying the experience.

These are wild animals in the open ocean, so the sharks are always treated with respect and safety comes first.

### I’m excited about shark diving, but also a little nervous. Is that normal?

Absolutely. For many guests, getting into the open ocean with sharks is well outside their normal comfort zone.

The crew will explain what to expect before you enter the water and stay with you throughout the experience. You’re never expected to do more than you’re comfortable doing.

### Do I need to be a strong swimmer or have diving experience?

No previous diving experience is required. Most of the experience takes place snorkeling at the surface, and the crew will assist you throughout the dive.

You should be comfortable floating and moving through the water, but you do not need to be an experienced swimmer, freediver, or scuba diver.

### Is this a scuba diving tour?

No. This is a snorkeling and freediving experience, not a scuba dive. No scuba certification is required, and snorkel equipment is provided.

### Are there any age, health, or experience requirements?

The minimum age is 5 years old with an adult, and no previous shark-diving or scuba experience is required.

Guests with serious health conditions should consult their physician before participating, particularly if they have concerns about being in the open ocean or climbing on and off the boat.

### What if I get seasick?

Seasickness can happen, especially for guests who are sensitive to boat motion.

If you know you’re prone to motion sickness, the crew strongly recommends preparing ahead of time with an appropriate over-the-counter motion-sickness remedy such as Dramamine or Bonine and following the medication directions.

## The Shark Diving Experience

### What makes Hawaii Adventure Diving different from other shark diving tours?

The goal is a more personal and immersive shark-diving experience rather than simply watching sharks from a distance.

Tours are limited to a maximum of six guests, and the team includes highly experienced shark divers, divemasters, freedivers, captains, marine professionals, and underwater photographers who spend significant time working with sharks in the open ocean.

### How close do we get to the sharks?

There is no fixed distance because every encounter is different and the sharks choose how they move through the water.

Sharks often come quite close. When conditions and shark behavior allow, the team gives guests the freedom to have a more immersive encounter rather than simply observing from one position. If you would rather keep more distance, that is completely fine too.

### What kinds of sharks might we see?

Common encounters can include Galapagos sharks, tiger sharks, hammerheads, and other pelagic shark species found around Oʻahu.

The exact animals you encounter vary because these are wild sharks in their natural environment.

### Will I definitely see sharks?

Sharks are wild animals, so wildlife encounters can never be guaranteed.

Every day in the ocean is different, and shark activity can vary with conditions. That unpredictability is also part of what makes seeing them in their natural environment so special.

### What happens on a typical tour?

The experience begins in Haleiwa with a boat ride roughly three miles offshore. During the ride, the crew explains what to expect, provides a safety briefing, reviews the equipment, and answers questions.

Once at the dive site, a safety diver enters the water first to evaluate conditions. Guests are then brought into the water for the shark encounter.

### How many people will be on the tour?

Tours are limited to a maximum of six guests.

The smaller group gives guests more room, more direct access to the crew, and more opportunity to enjoy the sharks without spending the experience crowded among a large group of swimmers.

### How long is the tour?

The complete experience is approximately two hours round trip from Haleiwa, including the boat ride to and from the dive site, safety briefing, preparation, and time in the water.

## Planning Your Tour

### What is included with the shark diving tour?

Your tour includes the approximately two-hour boat trip, safety briefing, experienced crew and safety divers, and the snorkel equipment needed for the dive.

Transportation to and from Haleiwa is not included.

### What should I bring?

Bring a valid photo ID, towel, reef-safe sunscreen, and a change of clothes if you’d like to be dry for the ride back.

A light jacket can also be useful because it may be windy on the boat. Sunglasses, a hat, and a waterproof camera such as a GoPro are welcome as well.

Snorkel gear is provided, so you do not need to bring your own unless you prefer to.

### What should I wear?

Wear swimwear that you’re comfortable snorkeling in.

You’ll be entering the open ocean directly from the boat, so choose something that stays secure and allows you to move comfortably in the water.

### Where does the tour leave from?

Tours depart from Haleiwa on Oʻahu’s North Shore.

Your booking confirmation should provide the exact meeting and check-in instructions for your tour.

### Can someone come along if they don’t want to get in the water?

Yes. Hawaii Adventure Diving offers a **Non-Diver Ride Along** option for guests who want to join the boat trip and experience the adventure without entering the water.

This can be a great option for couples, families, or groups where not everyone wants to shark dive.

## Weather, Changes & Cancellation

### What happens if the weather or ocean conditions are bad?

Ocean conditions can change, and safety always takes priority.

The captain and crew evaluate conditions before and during every trip and determine whether the tour can be operated safely. If conditions affect your scheduled tour, the team will work with you on the available options.

### What is the cancellation or rescheduling policy?

Hawaii Adventure Diving offers free cancellation up to 48 hours before your scheduled tour.

Cancellations within 48 hours of the tour and no-shows are charged the full tour amount.
